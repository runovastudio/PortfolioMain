Visual Synthesis

"I am deeply fascinated by procedural systems. To me, the mathematical iteration of a code block shares the exact same geometric soul as the repeating motifs woven into a traditional Anatolian rug."

My work is an eclectic synthesis of Eastern patterns and textures with Western game mechanics and 3D spaces. Bridging the gap between fine art reproductions, pixel-perfect technical execution, and interactive design.
