Roots: The 16th Century

Divan Poetry & Aruz Meter

The belief that aesthetics arise from intricate analysis finds its roots in 16th-century thought. The mathematical precision of aruz meter, analyzing syllables into patterns, forms the soul of my procedural approach. This is a tradition of seeing beauty in structure, a legacy I follow from masters like Bâkî.

Cihânı câm-ı nazmım şi'r-i Cringî gibi devr eyler

Bu bezmün şimdi biz de Bakî-i hem-demiz
