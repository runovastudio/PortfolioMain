The Ottoman Divan poetry

Cihânı câm-ı nazmım şi'r-i Cringî gibi devr eyler

Bu bezmün şimdi biz de Bakî-i hem-demiz

"My poetry turns the world like a wine-cup of verse;
In this era, we are those who share the breath of Bâkî."

The aruz meter used in Divan poetry is actually an unparalleled element of harmony. Syllables are classified according to their type, and an effort is made to adhere to this classification in all verses. My interest in Divan poetry directly influences my game design. This is because the goal of creating a holistic structure by analyzing the smallest building blocks, as seen in Divan poetry, is a tool I use in my game design. The syllable analysis I perform when writing poetry is the same analysis I apply to the ludemes (syllables) of the systems (poems) I build when making a game (divan). Knowing how powerfully the smallest ludeme (ludeme: we can call it the smallest building block of a game; a die in backgammon is an example) can affect the game's design, I can say that I have a game design philosophy that focuses on small design differences and starts from tiny building blocks.
