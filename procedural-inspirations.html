RNG (Random Number Generation)

The heart of unpredictability. I use RNG for everything from calculating the value of items in a treasure chest (lootValue = base * rng(0.8, 1.2)) to determining critical hit chances (isCritical = rng(0, 100) < critChance). It's the digital equivalent of a dice roll, bringing life and variance to static systems.

Perlin Noise

Unlike pure randomness, Perlin Noise creates natural-looking, organic patterns. It's my go-to tool for generating procedural textures, creating varied terrain for levels, or even implementing realistic camera shake. By changing the seed, you get a completely different yet structurally similar result.
